Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Orca Enterprise provides mobile detailing, residential cleaning, commercial cleaning and transportation services. This policy explains the information collected when you use the Orca Enterprise app and website.

Information we collect

  • Booking information such as your name, phone number, email address, address or pickup location, preferred date and time, and service notes.
  • Payment-related booking information such as booking number, amount due and payment status. Lynk payment processing occurs through the Lynk payment service; Orca Enterprise does not collect your Lynk account password or PIN.
  • Staff account information such as name, phone number, email address and employee ID when you register as staff.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as session and request information.

How we use information

We use information to receive and manage service bookings, communicate about bookings, administer staff access and attendance, process or verify payment status, maintain security, and operate and improve the service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when needed to operate the platform or process a requested payment or service. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Payments

Orca Enterprise currently directs customers to Lynk for payment. A Lynk payment may remain marked pending verification until Orca Enterprise verifies the payment. Orca Enterprise does not claim automatic Lynk payment confirmation.

Data retention and security

We retain booking and staff information as needed to provide the service, maintain records and meet legal or operational requirements. We use reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect information, but no internet system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact Orca Enterprise to ask about personal information associated with your booking or staff account and request correction where appropriate.

Children

The service is intended for customers and authorized parents or guardians. Where a booking concerns a child, the parent or guardian should provide the information and consent required for the service.
